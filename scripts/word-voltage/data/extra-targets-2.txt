# Word Voltage: more common words, added for Challenge levels 201-500.
#
# Same bar as extra-targets.txt: everyday American English a family knows,
# nothing rude, nothing about fighting, drinking or drugs, no names, no
# abbreviations. Every word must also be in ENABLE, which is how typos are
# caught (lexicon.ts reports any that aren't).
#
# Mostly six to eight letters, because the eight-letter wheels from level 301
# need common base words and a lot of common words inside them, plus plain
# plurals and past tenses a child would say.
