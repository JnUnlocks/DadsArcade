# Word Voltage theme: Dad's Garage.
#
# Engines, tools, tires and the classic cruisers. A nod to the great old
# American cars comes through ordinary dictionary words (CORVETTE, IMPALA,
# NOVA and BLAZER are a ship, an antelope, a star and a jacket there). No car
# maker's name or logo appears anywhere: the arcade keeps no trademarks.
#
# One scene per level: "== Title", then its words, themed words only. Only a
# scene's own words glow, and a board is built on one of them or carries
# three, so each scene leads with a good wheel word.
# Words must be in ENABLE, three to eight letters.
